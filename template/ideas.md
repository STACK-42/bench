# Ideas

The backlog of ideas to test. The `scouter` agent adds cards from the web and from books; anyone can add one by hand. Researchers and the main agent change the status after they test an idea, and add the experiment or report that decided it.

```text
## I-NNN short name (status)
Source: link, or "own idea"
Idea: one or two sentences.
Why here: which part of the score it should help, and what about our data suggests it.
Cost: hours to test; new libraries; external data (yes/no, and is it allowed?).
Result: (after testing) the numbers, and where they are recorded.
```

Status is one of `new`, `testing`, `kept`, `dropped`. A dropped card stays here, so nobody tries the same idea again without a new reason.
